# Example — Brenda, "who showed me this" reaction

A complete, real run of the skill. Read this before your first run; it is faster
than reading the templates cold, because you can see what a filled-in section is
supposed to look like.

- **`TEMPLATE.md`** — the system. Decode of two reference clips, the three
  non-negotiables, lane check, format spec, architecture diagram, fixed-reveal
  shot tables, and an 18-hook bank grouped by angle.
- **`SCRIPTS.md`** — the output. 11 numbered scripts (B1–B11), each four lines,
  plus the batch shooting order.

## What to notice

**The seam.** One line does all the work: *the text-overlay hook changes every
video, the reveal stays the same.* Everything downstream is a consequence of it.

**The fixed beat is written to be filmable blind.** §5 of the template is a timed
shot table, not a description. Someone who has never seen the references could
shoot it.

**The scripts do not repeat the reveal.** It appears once at the top of
`SCRIPTS.md` and every script after that just says `→ FIXED REVEAL A`. That
repetition-free structure is what makes the leverage visible at a glance — you
can count that 8 scripts share 1 shoot.

**Non-negotiables are physical.** "You see the keyboard, the ceiling, a little
shake" — a rough cut can be checked against that. "Make it feel authentic"
cannot.

**Two reveals, not five.** Reveal A (finished wall) is the default; Reveal B
(watch it build) is the alt for when you want to imply *how easy* it was. Each
additional fixed beat costs a shoot day, so they earn their place or they don't
exist.

## Reference clips

Not included in this repo — they're other creators' videos. The decode table in
`TEMPLATE.md` §1 captures everything the process needs from them. When you run
the skill yourself, drop your references in `samples/` inside your working folder
and keep them out of git.
