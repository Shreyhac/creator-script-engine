# Brenda — "Who Showed Me This" Reaction Template
### Skill (hero): Static Ads for Meta & Google (`static-ads`) · alt reveal: Product Photoshoot

> **The one rule:** the **text-overlay hook changes every video, the reveal stays the same.**
> Brenda never talks. She *reacts*. A trending sound + one burned-in line carry the hook, then a hard cut to her phone filming a laptop shows the payoff. Batch the reactions, reuse one reveal.

---

## 1. What the two samples actually are (decoded, 95%+ confidence)

Both reference clips (`samples/1.mp4` travel, `samples/2.mp4` design) are the **exact same template**:

| Beat | Sample 1 (Alexandra / travel) | Sample 2 (Shelby / design) |
|---|---|---|
| **0–3s — Swoon hook** | Face to camera, hand over mouth, giggling. Overlay: *"I could KISS the flight attendant who showed me this"* | Face to camera, smirk → hand-over-mouth giggle. Overlay: *"I could MARRY the graphic designer who showed me this site"* |
| **3s → end — Reveal** | Hard cut. **Phone films a laptop** (keyboard + room visible, handheld, slightly shaky): Google Flights LA→Tokyo cheap fare → Trips.com "Skyscanner Official Partner" → "$22 Toronto↔NY / $187 Miami↔Tokyo" deals wall | Hard cut. **Phone films a laptop**: `playgroundai.com` showing a **wall of finished, agency-grade logos** (Lumina Beauty, Spill Club, Hot Girls Read…), slow pan across them |
| Length | ~13s | ~9s |
| Voice | **None.** Trending audio only. | **None.** Trending audio only. |
| Answer to "who showed me this?" | the product itself — **URL visible on screen** (trips.com) | the product itself — **URL visible on screen** (playgroundai.com) |

**The three things that make this template work — do not break them:**
1. **Silent swoon.** Mouth stays closed (or covered). No VO, no explaining. The *face* sells "I can't believe this exists." This is Brenda's whole differentiator vs Candace (who talks).
2. **The organic phone-on-laptop reveal.** It is deliberately NOT a clean screen recording. You see the keyboard, the ceiling, a little shake. That's what sells *"a real person just found this and had to show me."* A polished screen-cap kills it.
3. **The product name lives on screen, never in a hard CTA.** The URL in frame is the payoff. It's native and soft, not a "comment X" money-hack. That softness is the point.

## 3. Format spec

- **Vertical 9:16**, phone-shot.
- **Silent.** Trending swoon/giggle audio + burned-in text. No voiceover.
- **Length: SHORT, ~9–14s** (match the samples — fast).
- **Two-beat build:** Swoon hook (0–3s) → Reveal (3s→end). One hard cut between them.
- **Reveal = phone films a laptop.** Keep it handheld and organic. `masonry.so` (or Claude + Masonry MCP) visible on the laptop.
- **CTA: soft.** Default = none; the URL on screen does the work. Optional low-key end caption "masonry.so 🤫" or, if a harder pull is wanted, "comment ADS 👇" (keeps Candace's keyword consistent for the DM automation).

## 6. HOOK BANK (the swappable first line)

Format: **"I could \[VERB] the \[PERSON] who showed me this \[THING]."** Burn it big, centered, first 3s, held into the reveal.

- **\[PERSON]** for the Static Ads reveal → **graphic designer** (best fit — the twist is there was no designer). Swap-ins for variety: *designer · ad agency · marketing agency · art director · whoever built this*. For the Product Photoshoot reveal → **photographer**.
- **\[THING]** → *this · this site · this app · this*.
